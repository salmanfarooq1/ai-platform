## Reading
- cProfile (before): validate_extensions -> jsonschema check_schema took ~90% of decision-write CPU.
- After: throughput 29 -> 47 decisions/s at 8 and 32 callers; serial p50 30.5 -> 23.9 ms.
- Throughput stays flat as callers grow: one tenant's decisions are written in hash-chain order, so they
  serialize on the chain head. Remaining time is mostly database wait. A later sprint can measure several tenants.
